Declaration of generative AI and AI-assisted technologies in the writing process

During the preparation of this work, the authors used Cursor (AI-assisted
coding and writing environment with large language model assistance) in order
to polish English phrasing, restructure selected sentences for clarity, and
assist with LaTeX formatting. After using this tool, the authors reviewed and
edited the content as needed and take full responsibility for the content of
the published article. No generative AI system was used to invent experimental
data, numerical results, tables, or scientific figures; the graphical abstract
and manuscript figures were produced with conventional plotting code under
author control.
