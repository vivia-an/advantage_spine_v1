Data availability

Audited seed-level factorial results, learning-rate audit records, support
identity measurements including the full cross-seed specificity matrix,
positive/negative-channel overlap and tail-concentration diagnostics,
Qwen3-8B primary-comparison and Qwen3-1.7B per-seed integer benchmark counts,
three-seed mask controls,
stability diagnostics, transfers, efficiency summaries, analysis scripts, and
figure generators are included in the
accompanying repository: https://github.com/vivia-an/advantage_spine_v1

The repository ships verify_numbers.py, which reconstructs benchmark rates
from the released integer correct/total records and recomputes all released
seed-derived means, sample standard deviations, paired contrasts, and cross-file
consistency checks. Synthetic schema fixtures and reconstructed manifests are
not released as experimental evidence. The public mask evidence consists of
author-attested seed-level support metrics, not the remote raw mask files or
byte-level identities. Checkpoints are available only
when redistribution is permitted by the corresponding upstream model and data
terms. LICENSE_AND_DATA_USE.md records the resource-level license and
redistribution boundary. In particular, the artifact does not redistribute
MATH500 or AIME 2024 problem text because the referenced dataset cards do not
declare a license; it releases their hashes, identities, counts, and aggregate
results instead.
