Data availability

Audited seed-level factorial results, learning-rate audit records, support
identity measurements including the full cross-seed specificity matrix,
Qwen3-1.7B per-seed benchmark decomposition, mask controls, stability
diagnostics, transfer
aggregates, analysis scripts, and figure generators are included in the
accompanying repository: https://github.com/vivia-an/advantage_spine_v1

The repository ships verify_numbers.py, which recomputes all factorial means,
sample standard deviations, the paired confidence interval, principal
contrasts, and cross-file consistency checks. It also includes the normalized
cross-cluster audit export: run identities, protocol metadata, reported
artifact digests, corrected timestamps, and a locally recomputed package
manifest. PROVENANCE.json distinguishes source-reported digests from files
available for local byte-level verification. Checkpoints are available only
when redistribution is permitted by the corresponding upstream model and data
terms. LICENSE_AND_DATA_USE.md records the resource-level license and
redistribution boundary. In particular, the artifact does not redistribute
MATH500 or AIME 2024 problem text because the referenced dataset cards do not
declare a license; it releases their hashes, identities, counts, and aggregate
results instead.
